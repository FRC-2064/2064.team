**Who:** all rookie members. **Deadline:** January 1.

This is the path from no CAD experience to a design-subteam contribution. You pass by submitting an Onshape document at the end of each stage, not by a quiz. Finish Module 1.2 by January 1 to be eligible for the March travel team. Veterans can use it as a refresher or go to Phase 2.

:::danger
January 1 is a hard deadline for rookies who want to travel. The stages are in order. Do not skip a prerequisite.
:::

## Before Stage 0

FIRST's [Module 3: Intro to CAD and 3D Printing](https://training.firstinspires.org/courses/module-3-intro-to-cad-and-3d-printing) is about two and a half hours. It covers how FRC teams use CAD and printers. FIRST recommends it before kickoff. It is the overview. The stages below are the 2064 credential, in Onshape.

Dashboard login if you want the credit stored. For modeling strategy beyond these stages, use the [FRCDesign.org learning course](https://www.frcdesign.org/).
