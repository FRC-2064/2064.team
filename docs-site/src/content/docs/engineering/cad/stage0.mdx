Welcome to the CAD pathway. Team 2064 uses Onshape, a cloud-based CAD platform, so you can design in the browser.

For this first step, follow the FRCDesign.org setup, then come back here to submit your progress.

## Step 1: Complete the setup guide

Keep this tab open. Open the FRCDesign setup guide and complete only the steps in the Course Setup section.

:::caution[Account]
When you sign up for Onshape, choose the free **Education** account and use your Region 15 email address.
:::

[Open the FRCDesign new-to-CAD setup](https://www.frcdesign.org/learning-course/course-setup/new-to-cad/)

## Step 2: Return checklist

Confirm the external guide is finished before you start sketching.

- [ ] Created a free Onshape Education account
- [ ] Logged in successfully
- [ ] Set default units to **inches** and **pounds**
- [ ] Subscribed to the **FRC Design** app in the Onshape App Store
- [ ] Added the standard **FeatureScripts** to your toolbar

## Step 3: Open your workbench

Use the 2064 Onshape Classroom so your document sits in the team folder and mentors can review it.

1. Open the Stage 0 portal.
2. Launch or generate your working document. Use that same document to verify setup and load FeatureScripts.
3. A mentor will check your interface settings from the classroom link.

[Enter the Stage 0 workbench](https://cad.onshape.com/classes/69ef9246c70581c5f4ff7cbd/assignment/69ef9260a120849efc8bbfa0)
