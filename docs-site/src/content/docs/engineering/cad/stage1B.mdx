> **Objective:** Learn FRC power transmission — motors, shafts, gears, belts, and chains — and model three custom gearboxes in Onshape.

:::note[Prerequisites]

:::danger[Stop]
If you have not completed the module above, you do not have the clearance to begin this task.
:::

So far you have designed structural parts. A robot also needs power. This module covers how rotational motion moves from a motor to a mechanism through gears, belts, and chains.

## Step 1: Open your workbench

Gearbox models need library parts: motors, hex shafts, and gears. Use the classroom workbench so those parts are already loaded.

1. Open the Stage 1B workbench.
2. Launch your working document from the classroom link. Build the gear, belt, and chain drives in that file.

## Step 2: Complete the training exercises

Work through these sections in order. Read the theory before the exercises. FRC hardware, including hex shafts, is specific.

## Step 3: Return checklist

- [ ] Identify common FRC motors (Kraken, Falcon, NEO)
- [ ] Model a **1/2 in hex shaft** and use spacers for alignment
- [ ] Use the **FRC Design** FeatureScript to generate gears with the correct tooth count
- [ ] Calculate center-to-center distances for belts and chains
- [ ] Use mate connectors to align motion components

## Step 4: Submission

1. Click **Submit** in the Onshape Classroom.
2. Tell a CAD mentor or your subteam lead. They will check gear ratios and center-to-center distances from your workbench link.
