This is the last stage of basic CAD training. You can sketch, extrude, and transfer power. Here you model real robot subsystems.

## Step 1: Open the practice exercises

This stage has **8 CAD exercises**. Use the classroom workbench so mentors can review your layout sketches and assembly logic.

1. Open the Stage 1C workbench.
2. Review the 8 mechanism outlines, then complete them one at a time.

[Enter the Stage 1C workbench](https://cad.onshape.com/classes/69ef9246c70581c5f4ff7cbd/assignment/69ef943ec1d9b3aafabc7aaa)

## Step 2: Design standards

Read the FRCDesign introduction before you model. It covers two Team 2064 standards: layout sketches (one master sketch drives the model) and consistent origins.

[Open the Stage 1C introduction](https://www.frcdesign.org/learning-course/stage1/1C/introduction/)

## Step 3: Return checklist

- [ ] Every mechanism has a layout sketch on the Top or Front plane
- [ ] Assemblies use standard content (bolts, nuts, and spacers) where it applies
- [ ] Motion is limited with mate limits (an elevator slide cannot leave its track)
- [ ] The assembly origin is centered so subsystems can be mirrored

## Step 4: Final checkout

1. Name every part, and fully constrain every sketch (black lines, no blue).
2. Click **Submit** in the Onshape Classroom.
3. Tell a CAD mentor or your subteam lead that the portfolio is ready for Level 1 CAD sign-off.
