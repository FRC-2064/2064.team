So far you have modeled isolated parts and simple gearboxes. This stage is a real robot workflow: how Team 2064 manages a multi-part assembly without the model breaking.

## Step 1: Open your workbench

This project needs the master layout templates and the pre-modeled scoring mechanisms. Use the classroom workbench so those assets load correctly.

1. Open the Stage 1D workbench.
2. Launch or generate your project document. That file is the master assembly for the drivebase.

[Enter the Stage 1D workbench](https://cad.onshape.com/classes/69ef9246c70581c5f4ff7cbd/assignment/69efb4132cc81a32f19fa461)

## Step 3: Return checklist

- [ ] The drivetrain frame is driven by the master layout sketch
- [ ] Changing one layout-sketch dimension updates the tubes and module positions
- [ ] COTS swerve modules are mated and organized in subassemblies
- [ ] The top-level assembly has no red or yellow mate errors

## Step 4: Submission

1. Click **Submit** in the Onshape Classroom.
2. Tell a CAD mentor. They will check layout constraints and confirm the elevator is integrated into the chassis.
