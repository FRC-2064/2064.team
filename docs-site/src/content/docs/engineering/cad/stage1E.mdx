If you have not completed the module above, you do not have the clearance to begin this task.

Stage 1D built the structural frame. A frame is not a robot until it has electronics and bumpers. This stage is the detailing pass: skeleton to competition-ready machine.

## Step 1: Open your detailing workbench

This module uses the drivebase from Stage 1D. Use the classroom document so the electronics libraries and bumper templates are available.

1. Open the Stage 1E workbench.
2. Launch your project document. Add the battery mount, electronics layout, and bumper assemblies there.

[Enter the Stage 1E workbench](https://cad.onshape.com/classes/69ef9246c70581c5f4ff7cbd/assignment/69efb44ac70581c5f419af92)

## Step 2: Complete the detailing exercises

Work through these sections in order. Read the theory first. Battery mounting and bumper size are rules items, not style choices.

## Step 3: Return checklist

- [ ] The battery cannot fall out in a tip
- [ ] Electronics are imported from MKCAD and fastened to the bellypan
- [ ] Pocketing saves weight, and solid material remains under every electronics mounting hole
- [ ] Bumpers follow FRC rules: 3/4 in plywood, 2.5 in noodles, and secure mounting pins

## Step 4: Submission

1. Click **Submit** in the Onshape Classroom.
2. Tell a CAD mentor. They will check for interference between the electronics and the battery mount.
