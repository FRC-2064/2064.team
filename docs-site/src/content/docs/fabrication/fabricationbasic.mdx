> **Objective:** Learn the foundational hand tools, basic power tools, and shop organization skills required to work safely in the Panther Project shop.

Welcome to the Team 2064 machine shop! Before you can build a 125-pound competition robot, you need to prove you have mastered the absolute basics.

:::danger
**Mandatory Rookie Requirement**  
**STOP!** You **MUST** complete and pass both Level 1 and Level 2 certifications before you are allowed to operate *any* other tool or piece of machinery in the shop. There are no exceptions to this rule. You cannot move on to bandsaws, CNC routers, or heavy machinery until you have mastered the basics.
:::

## What to Expect

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="🟢 Level 1 — General Shop" icon="document">
    Fundamentals of workspace navigation, organization, and safe hand tool use.

    - 🧹 **Workspace Organization** — Closet management, battery care, and keeping the shop clean  
    - 🔩 **Fasteners** — Identifying bolts, nuts, and rivets used on our robots  
    - 🔧 **Hand Tools** — Measuring tools, wrenches, screwdrivers, Allen keys, and basic assembly  
    - ⚡ **Wiring & Crimping** — Stripping wires and making secure connections  
    - 📝 **Skill Check** — Assessment to prove hand-tool proficiency before advancing
  </Card>

  <Card title="🟡 Level 2 — Power Tools & Basics" icon="setting">
    Basic powered equipment and electronics fabrication (after passing Level 1).

    - 🔋 **Basic Power Tools** — Cordless drills and impact drivers (without stripping screws)  
    - 🔌 **Soldering** — Techniques for permanent, secure FRC electronics connections  
    - 📝 **Skill Check** — Final Phase 1 fabrication assessment
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How to Advance

To complete this module, work through each lesson in order, read the documentation, and physically demonstrate your skills to the Fabrication Lead or a Mentor.

Once you pass Level 2, you will be cleared to use these tools on the competition robot and unlock Level 3 and 4 machinery in the Phase 2 Specialization Pathways.
