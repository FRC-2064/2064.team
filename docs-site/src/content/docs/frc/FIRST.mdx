## What this is

**FIRST** builds people, not just robots. FRC is the high-school program within FIRST. Each January it releases a game. Teams have about six weeks to design, build, and drive a robot, then play a three-day event: inspection, assigned alliances, then playoffs.

We wear the number **2064** which is Pomperaug High School's designated FRC team number. Pomperaug students have worn the number since 2006. The forward facing public team name is **The Panther Project** our robotics team can be identified as 2064 at events with other robots, but in public we use the name The Panther Project, or Pomperaug Robotics Team. 

Please Complete this training module provided by FIRST.
