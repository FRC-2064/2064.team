## Who we are

**FIRST** builds people, not just robots. FRC is the high-school program. Each January it releases a game. Teams have about six weeks to design, build, and drive a robot, then play a three-day event: inspection, assigned alliances, then playoffs.

The number on the robot is **2064**. Pomperaug students have worn it since 2006. At an event, other teams find us by that number. In public the name is **The Panther Project**, or the Pomperaug Robotics Team.
