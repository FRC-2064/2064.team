## What this is

**FIRST** builds people, not just robots. FRC is the high-school program within FIRST. Each January it releases a game. Teams have about six weeks to design, build, and drive a robot, then play a three-day event: inspection, assigned alliances, then playoffs.

We wear the number **2064** which is Pomperaug High School's designated FRC team number. Pomperaug students have worn the number since 2006. The forward facing public team name is **The Panther Project** our robotics team can be identified as 2064 at events with other robots, but in public we use the name The Panther Project, or Pomperaug Robotics Team. 

Please Complete this training module provided by FIRST.

## How 2064 competes

**Gracious Professionalism** is full effort and decent conduct at the same time. Play to win. Do not trash-talk a team, a driver, or a school. In our shop, disagree, then do the job the lead assigned. Return a borrowed tool the same day.

**Coopertition** is sharing without handing over the match. A form-ready case: our robot has passed inspection, and the team next to us cannot seat a battery connector. Loan the cable. Still play them at full strength. Do not give them our match plan, and do not leave our own robot down to do their repair.

Teams remember who loaned the cable. That is how the Panther Project gets picked, and how we can ask for help later.
