**Objective:** Know what January is for, what a new member is expected to do, and what stop build means.

:::danger[STOP]
Build season does not skip safety clearance. A busy shop is not a reason to enter the build area, power a robot, or use a tool you are not signed off on.
:::

## What it is

Build season is the window after kickoff. Kickoff is the first Saturday in January, when school is back from winter break. *FIRST* reveals the game that morning. Every team reads the same manual at the same time.

Team 2064 then has about six weeks to turn that manual into a robot that can play. The deadline is stop-build day. After that date the competition robot is frozen for the event it is entered in. Practice and pit fixes still happen. A new mechanism after stop build usually does not.

Fall meetings and the XRP are how you learn the shop. Build season is when the game is real and the clock is short.

## The six weeks

| When | The shop | A new member |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kickoff Saturday | Reveal, manual, scoring argument | Opinions are welcome. Invented rules are not. |
| Week 1 | Musts, wants, prototypes on the table | The robot is not supposed to exist yet. |
| Weeks 2–3 | Prove mechanisms in wood or PLA; lock the frame | A failed prototype is the work, not a delay. |
| Weeks 3–4 | Cut, assemble, and wire | The shop gets loud. Bumpers start only after the perimeter is real. |
| Weeks 5–6 | One robot, match cycles, drivers, then freeze | A new idea that is not on the robot yet is probably too late. |

A later module owns shop nights and who owns each subsystem. If that schedule and this table disagree, follow the later one.

---

## What to expect

* More hours. After-school nights and some weekends. School still comes first. Tell a lead early if you cannot make a night.
* A subteam, not a free roam. You will be placed. Ask to try another area after you have been useful on the first.
* No job is normal for a day, not for a week. Ask a lead. First jobs that do not need a tool sign-off: cleanup, part sorting, bumper help, writing down what a prototype test broke.
* Week 2 is supposed to look unfinished. Do not judge the season by the first prototype.
* Being in the room does not clear you on the mill, the CNC, or enabling the robot.
