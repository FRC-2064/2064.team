## What REBUILT is

Two alliances score **FUEL** into their **HUB** and climb their **TOWER**. Fuel scored in the hub comes back onto the field.

<Steps>
1. **Auto, 20 seconds.** Both hubs are active. Preload, score, pick up, climb to Level 1.

2. **Teleop.** Hubs take turns. Fuel in an inactive hub does not count.

3. **Endgame, last 30 seconds.** Both hubs are active. Climb, and try to clear the ranking-point lines.
</Steps>

Point values at kickoff. Confirm Table 6-4. Team updates changed some of these.

| Action | Auto | Teleop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FUEL in an active HUB | 1 | 1 |
| FUEL in an inactive HUB | 0 | 0 |
| TOWER Level 1 | 15 | 10 |
| TOWER Level 2 | — | 20 |
| TOWER Level 3 | — | 30 |

A win is 3 ranking points. Bonus RP came from fuel thresholds and a tower-point threshold. Look those up in Table 6-5.

Design limits, not suggestions:

* Starting size at or under 110 in perimeter and 30 in tall. Could not extend above 30 in, and only out of one side at a time.
* Robot plus bumpers at or under 135 lb.
* Bumpers cover the frame, fill 2.75 to 5.5 in off the floor, and show 2064 in white on at least three sides.
* Cross the field over the bumps or under the trenches.

## Need, then shop

Separate rules from wishes before the form. High means design time, spares, and drive practice. Medium ships only if the High items already work.

| Subsystem | Job | Priority | Shop question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Drivetrain | Cross bumps or the trench and reset | High | Does swerve clear the trench and stay planted on the bump? |
| Intake | Floor, depot, or human player | High | What bumper gap does that need? |
| Storage | Hold fuel inside the 30 in cap | Medium | How many balls fit without jamming? |
| Scorer | Deliver into the active hub | High | What happens when the hub is inactive? |
| Climber | Level 1, 2, or 3 without going over 30 in early | High | Which level is real for our weight and our drivers? |

A mechanism the shop cannot wire, code, and drive before the first event is a risk, not a High priority.
