Welcome to **The Panther Project**! Before you can design or build a 125-pound competition robot, you need to master our engineering baselines. Phase 1 is your technical onboarding bootcamp. 

In this phase, you will explore the culture of FIRST Robotics, pass critical machine shop safety certifications, master fundamental CAD structures, and learn hand-tool workflows. You will cap it all off by assembling, wiring, and programming a miniature XRP Starter Bot.

:::danger[Crucial Progress Rule]
Work through these training modules sequentially. You **must** complete and pass the **Safety Certification (Module 1.3)** before you are permitted to handle any physical fabrication tools or start Modules 1.4 and 1.5.
:::

---

## 🗺️ Your Training Modules

### 🤖 1.1: FRC Basics
> **[ 🟢 Self-Paced ]** > Learn the culture. Get an overview of FIRST, break down robot anatomy, analyze past games, and learn what to expect during Build Season.  
> 🔗 [**Start Intro to FIRST →**](/frc/FIRST/)

<br>

### 💻 1.2: Basic CAD
> **[ 🔒 Prerequisite: Module 1.1 ]** > Learn to think in 3D. Master the Engineering Design Process and set up your Onshape account for core mechanism modeling.  
> 🔗 [**Start Stage 0 (New to CAD) →**](/engineering/cad/CAD/)

<br>

### 🛡️ 1.3: Safety & Certification
> **[ ⚠️ Critical Path ]** > Learn core machine shop safety, power tool rules, and pass both your written quiz and your 1.3.4 Safety Practical test.  
> 🔗 [**Start General Safety →**](/safety/safetylanding/)

<br>

### 🛠️ 1.4: Fabrication
> **[ 🔒 Prerequisite: Module 1.3 ]** > Get hands-on. Learn how to organize the workspace, utilize hand tools safely, identify fasteners, and master electronics crimping and soldering.  
> 🔗 [**Start Workspace Org →**](/fabrication/fabricationbasic/)

<br>

### 🏎️ 1.5: The XRP Bot
> **[ 🔒 Prerequisite: Modules 1.2 & 1.3 ]** > Put it all together. Assemble, wire, and program a functional mini-robot using WPILib and Java to prove your baseline engineering skills.  
> 🔗 [**Start Build Guide →**](/xrprobotics/xrplanding/)
