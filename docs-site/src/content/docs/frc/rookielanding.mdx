**Mandatory** for incoming members of the Panther Project, FRC Team 2064.

Phase 1 is the shared floor: what *FIRST* is, how a robot is put together, shop clearance, basic CAD, hand tools, and a small XRP robot. You do not need to finish CAD before you start safety. You do need safety clearance before you use a shop tool.

:::danger
Start safety while you are in 1.1 and 1.2. Do not wait until CAD is done. Module 1.3 has to be passed before fabrication tools, and before the hands-on parts of 1.4 and 1.5.
:::
