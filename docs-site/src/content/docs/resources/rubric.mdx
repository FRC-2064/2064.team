> **Engineering Excellence & Character:** Use this rubric to evaluate your progress and skill development throughout your training. These standards reflect the **FIRST® Core Values** that drive the Panther Project.

---

## 🧭 Core Values Reference

| Value | FRC Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Discovery** | Exploring new skills and ideas; valuing the learning process over the final trophy. |
| **Innovation** | Using creativity and persistence to solve complex engineering challenges. |
| **Impact** | Applying technical skills to improve our local and global communities. |
| **Inclusion** | Embracing diverse perspectives and ensuring every team member has a seat at the table. |
| **Teamwork** | Collaborative problem-solving and supporting both teammates and opposing alliances (Coopertition). |
| **Fun** | Celebrating hard work and finding genuine joy in the engineering process. |

---

## 📝 Self-Reflection Guide

The goal is to align with these values for every task you undertake. Remember that *even with the smallest task*, not everything will be immediately familiar. We are all perpetual learners—our curiosity keeps us engaged and seeking answers. 

These values connect your daily progress to the team's greater mission: **building well-rounded, capable members who go on to enrich the community with the skills and knowledge gained on FRC Team 2064.**

---

## 📊 Evaluation Matrix

| Category | Level 1: Beginning | Level 2: Progressing | Level 3: Approaching | Level 4: Meeting (Standard) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Discovery** | Relies entirely on others to initiate tasks and discover solutions; rarely explores independently. | Approaches tasks but requires frequent assistance and guidance to reach a point of discovery. | Asks targeted questions of peers or mentors while persevering to discover answers independently. | Independently investigates all available resources, utilizing deep persistence to discover solutions. |
| **Innovation** | Struggles with creative problem-solving, relies strictly on given instructions, and requires ongoing encouragement. | Applies basic creativity but occasionally struggles with perseverance when technical blocks occur. | Employs creativity and persistence to solve problems independently, developing viable alternative approaches. | Leverages high creativity and resilience to engineer unique, optimized solutions to complex tasks. |
| **Impact** | Completes tasks mechanically without considering their broader application or future value. | Understands technical requirements but struggles to connect how the work impacts personal growth or others. | Connects daily tasks to personal growth, recognizing the long-term value of the acquired skills. | Actively applies technical learning with a clear vision of how it impacts personal growth and helps others. |
| **Inclusion** | Works in isolation; solutions do not account for or incorporate diverse viewpoints or accessibility needs. | Occasionally seeks out others' input; attempts to understand differing views with partial integration. | Regularly incorporates peers' ideas and differing perspectives, successfully addressing team needs. | Seamlessly embraces diverse viewpoints, designing solutions and workflows centered around inclusivity and collaboration. |
| **Teamwork** | Inconsistently applies collaboration, communication, or project management habits. | Utilizes basic collaboration and communication to complete individual assigned duties. | Effectively uses communication and project management tools to coordinate tasks across the subteam. | Exemplifies elite collaboration, proactive communication, and leadership to drive group success. |
| **Fun** | Focuses solely on difficulties, viewing tasks as a burden rather than a learning opportunity. | Maintains a positive attitude during straightforward tasks but struggles when technical challenges arise. | Sustains a positive mindset through technical roadblocks, finding satisfaction in the problem-solving process. | Actively fosters an energetic, joyful shop environment and finds genuine enthusiasm in technical challenges. |
