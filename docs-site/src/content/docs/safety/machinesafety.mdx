**Objective:** Know the machines used for the safety practical, and only those. A mentor demos each one. The doc page is the reference, not the sign-off.

:::danger
This does not clear you on the mill, the lathe, the CNC, or the wood bandsaw. Those are later pathway training. Watching a mentor is not permission to start a spindle.
:::

## What a mentor will show you

Enough to make the practical part: mark it, cut it, drill it, clean the edges.

* Reading and marking parts for manufacturing.
* Cordless bandsaw, stock in a vise, not in your hand.
* Horizontal cutoff bandsaw.
* Sander, after the cut for clean edges.
* Cordless drill, and drilling a clamped part accurately.

Glasses stay on. You do not start one until the mentor has checked the setup.
