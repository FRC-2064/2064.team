title: "1.3.4 - Safety Practical"
---

**Objective:** Make one part, in front of a mentor, without skipping a safety step. The drawing is the assignment. This page is the procedure.

:::danger
A mentor is in the room for the whole practical. One student or a small group. You do not start a machine until that mentor says the setup is ready. Gloves stay off the saw, the sander, and the drill.
:::

Glasses on. Hair tied back. Closed-toe shoes. The part is not a pass if the cleanup is skipped.

## The drawing

The mentor gives you the sketch. Cut length, hole size, and hole location come from that sheet, not from memory.

![Part drawing](/img/safetypractical.png)

If the image is missing, use the paper copy on the bench. Do not guess a dimension.

## Do this in order

1. **Read the task.** Say the cut length, the hole size, and the hole location back to the mentor before you touch stock.
2. **Get the material.** The stock on the drawing, not a leftover that is close.
3. **Get the tools.** Marker, square or rule, the saw you were assigned, sander, drill and the bit that matches the hole, and a vise or clamp.
4. **Pick a space.** Clear bench. Cord not across the walk. Someone can stand beside you without being in the cut path.
5. **Mark the cut.** Measure from a clean end. Mark the waste side so the finished length is still there after the kerf.
6. **Cut.** Mentor checks the setup. Then one of these:
   * Vertical bandsaw, part supported, hands clear of the blade.
   * Cordless saw, stock held in a vise, not in your hand.
   * Horizontal bandsaw, stock clamped, guard in place.
7. **Stop the blade before you reach in.** Wait until it is still.
8. **Sand the edges.** Sander off until the part is on the table. Deburr the cut and the corners the drawing calls out. No gloves.
9. **Mark the hole.** Location from the drawing, measured from the edges you just cut, not from the scrap.
10. **Drill.** Clamp the part. Bit matches the callout. Mentor checks the clamp and the bit. Drill, then stop the chuck before you move the part.
11. **Clean up.** Tools back. Scrap in the bin. Bench brushed. Floor clear. Battery unplugged or saw unplugged if you used one.

## Rubric

A mentor marks this. Every row has to pass. One miss is a redo of that step, not a debate.

| Step | Pass | Not a pass |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief | States cut length, hole size, and hole location before starting | Starts from memory |
| PPE | Glasses, shoes, hair, no gloves on rotating tools | Fixes it only after being told twice |
| Setup | Stock and bit match the drawing; bench is clear | Wrong stock, wrong bit, or a cord across the walk |
| Mark and cut | Mark is on the waste side; saw stops before hands enter | Cuts short, holds stock by hand on the cordless saw, or reaches in early |
| Edges | Cut edge deburred; part is safe to hand over | Sharp edge left on the cut |
| Hole | Size and location match the drawing; part was clamped | Handheld part, wrong bit, or hole in the wrong face |
| Cleanup | Tools, scrap, and floor done before the mentor is asked to grade | Leaves the bench for someone else |

The part passes when the mentor signs the sheet and the bench is the way you found it.
