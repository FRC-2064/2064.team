**Objective:** Make one part, in front of a mentor, without skipping a safety step. The drawing is the assignment. This page is the order.

:::danger
A mentor is in the room for the whole practical. One student or a small group. You do not start a machine until that mentor says the setup is ready. Gloves stay off the saw, the sander, and the drill.
:::

Glasses on. Hair tied back. Closed-toe shoes. The part is not a pass if the cleanup is skipped.

## The drawing

Cut length, hole size, and hole location come from this sheet. Do not guess.

![Part drawing](/img/safetypractical.png)

Paper copy on the bench if the image does not load. Two holes: 3/8 centered through the 2-inch face, 1/4 through the 1-inch face and 0.50 from one end.

## Do this in order

<Steps>
1. **Read the task.** Say the cut length and both hole sizes and locations before you touch stock.

2. **Pick a space.** Clear bench. 

3. **Get the tools.** Marker, caliper, the saw you were assigned, drill, both bits, and a vise or clamp.

4. **Get the material.** The tube on the drawing, not a leftover that is close.

5. **Mark the cut.** Measure from a clean end. Mark the waste side so the 2.00 length is still there after the kerf.

6. **Cut.** Mentor checks the setup. Cordless saw with the stock in a vise, or the horizontal bandsaw with the stock clamped and the guard in place.

7. **Stop the blade.** Wait until it is still before you reach in.

8. **Sand the edges.** Sander off when not using, don't walk away from a spinning machine. Deburr the cut. No gloves.

9. **Mark the holes.** Measure from the edges you just cut. Center punch the 3/8 and the 1/4 hole in the right location.

10. **Drill.** Clamp the part in vise. Bit matches the callout. Mentor checks the clamp and the bit. Stop the chuck before you move the part. Then the other hole.

11. **Clean up.** Tools back. Scrap in the bin. Bench brushed. Floor clear. Battery on charger if it needs it.
</Steps>

## Rubric

A mentor marks this. Every row has to pass. One miss is a redo of that step.

| Step | Pass | Not a pass |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief | States length and both holes before starting | Starts from memory |
| PPE | Glasses, shoes, hair, no gloves on rotating tools | Fixes it only after being told twice |
| Setup | Stock and bits match the drawing; bench is clear | Wrong stock, wrong bit, or a cord across the walk |
| Mark and cut | Mark is on the waste side; saw stops before hands enter | Cuts short, holds stock by hand, or reaches in early |
| Edges | Cut edge deburred; part is safe to hand over | Sharp edge left on the cut |
| Holes | Both sizes and locations match; part was clamped | Handheld part, wrong bit, or hole in the wrong face |
| Cleanup | Tools, scrap, and floor done before the grade | Leaves the bench for someone else |
