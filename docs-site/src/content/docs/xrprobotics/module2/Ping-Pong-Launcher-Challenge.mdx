## Main Components of the Build

1. **WCD Drivetrain**
   * Modified frame for center axis driving similar to WCD 6 wheel drive FRC bot.
2. **Intake**
   * The primary over the bumper intake assembly for ball intake into hopper. 
3. **Hopper**
   * Ramped surface to hold up to 10 balls at a time.
4. **Superstructure**
   * Frame components for shooter assembly to hold battery/shooter motors etc.   
5. **Shooter Gate**
   * Mechanism that allows balls to move from the hopper into the flywheel path.
6. **Flywheel**
   * High-speed wheel made of TPU for shooting the foam balls.

---

Students are provided all 3D printed components as a kit, and will be able to modify and/or improve them if they want to. The game is designed to be completed with the default robot design and should be able to score points as a standard kitbot.
