> **Custom Electronics:** This step adds a small **Mossfet** as a sub-controller so the XRP can drive the high-RPM launcher motor.

:::caution
**Stop!**
Only continue if you have **completed the physical assembly** of the bot.  
If not, go back to the Assembly Guide first.
:::

:::note
**Prerequisites**
Your base XRP should already be built and wired.  
This is a custom add-on required for the high-RPM launcher motors. 
:::

## What You Will Be Doing

You are adding a small motor driver (MOSFET) so the launcher motors can spin a motor fast enough to shoot a ball.

The XRP does not power the launcher motors directly. It only sends a simple command to the MOSFET and enable the external 12v battery to power the motor.

:::note
**The FRC Big Picture**
This is a miniature version of how a real FRC robot works.

In competition robots, the RoboRIO / SystemCore (your XRP) does not power the motors directly. It sends a low-voltage signal to a smart motor controller (MOSFET). That controller then pulls high current straight from the battery to spin the motors.
:::

## Master Wiring Table

Follow this table exactly.

| Connection Group   | From (Component : Pin)             | To (Component : Pin)         | Logic/Voltage | Purpose                 |
| :----------------- | :--------------------------------- | :--------------------------- | :------------ | :---------------------- |
| **Control Signal** | **XRP SERVO2:** Signal (IO17)      | **Pro Micro:** Pin 2         | PWM           | Signal from WPILib      |
| **Common Ground**  | **L9110S:** Ground (GND)           | **XRP SERVO2:** Ground (GND) | 0V            | Completes the circuit   |
| **Arduino Power**  | **XRP SERVO2:** Power (Center/Red) | **Pro Micro:** RAW           | 5V            | Powers the Arduino      |
| **Motor A Drive**  | **Pro Micro:** Pin 5 & 6           | **L9110S:** A-1A & A-1B      | 5V Logic      | Speed/Direction A       |
| **Motor B Drive**  | **Pro Micro:** Pin 9 & 10          | **L9110S:** B-1A & B-1B      | 5V Logic      | Speed/Direction B       |
| **Motor Power**    | **XRP Battery:** Red Wire          | **L9110S:** VCC              | 6V Battery    | **High Current Supply** |


Once all wires match the table and the wiring diagram, you’re ready to continue.
